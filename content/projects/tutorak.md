Tutorak is a cutting-edge online tutoring platform that connects students with expert tutors for personalized learning experiences. Designed for both students and tutors, it provides a seamless interface for booking sessions, managing schedules, and real-time communication.

With a robust Learning Management System (LMS), students can access structured lessons, track their progress, and receive performance reports. Tutors can easily create profiles, manage bookings, and interact with students through live chat and video conferencing.

Built with Laravel, Vue.js, and MySQL, Tutorak ensures a smooth and efficient user experience, incorporating secure authentication, AI-powered search, and online payment integration for hassle-free transactions. Whether on desktop, tablet, or mobile, Tutorak makes high-quality education accessible, flexible, and interactive for learners worldwide.

---

### Features

- Online Tutoring Platform
- Session Scheduling
- Secure Authentication
- Learning Management System (LMS)
- Progress Tracking & Reports
- Real-time Communication
- Video Conferencing Integration
- Online Payment & Subscription
- AI-Powered Search & Recommendations
- Multi-Device Accessibility
