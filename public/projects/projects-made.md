It digitally connects the Central Sterile Services Department (CSSD) to caregiving units such as ICUs, Operating Rooms (OR); enabling full traceability, automatic identification, cycle tracking and documentation for all sterile supplies. Maintain inventory in real-time
Optimise sterility and life-cycle of instruments
Enable intra-department visibility
Reduce instances of lost or misplaced instruments

Privadoc

In this project users can generate Privacy, Cookie Policy and Terms & Conditions for their websites, mobile and Facebook apps. Also users can integrate advanced EU Cookie Banner and Consent.
This project provide solutions to make websites and apps compliant with the law across multiple countries and legislation. Different countries has different law. For example - GDPR, CCPA and LGPD, ePrivacy and Australia's APPs. So this project provide all countries law information. User can select laws accoring to his requirement. 
Also users need to be informed of website/app owner details, what data is being collected, third-party access to their data, their rights in regards to their data.

So in this project users can embed the laws in their website. There are different plans added with different features. User can buy a plan and can configure the laws for their website. 
Paypal and Stripe payment api is used for payment.
